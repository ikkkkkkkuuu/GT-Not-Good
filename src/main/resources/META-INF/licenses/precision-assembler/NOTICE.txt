src/main/java/com/xyp/gtnotgood/common/machines/multiblock/PrecisionAssembler.java
The multiblock layout, two processing modes, tier checks, parallel counts and
related tooltip wording are based on the upstream Precise Auto-Assembler.
The adaptation uses five GregTech machine casings, standard energy-hatch
amperage overclocking with two-hatch tier skipping, perfect overclocking and
an MV-stage controller recipe. Internal machine casings restrict the base
recipe tier, without restricting energy hatch tiers or overclocking voltage.
GregTech textures are referenced at runtime;
no upstream texture images are copied into this feature.
