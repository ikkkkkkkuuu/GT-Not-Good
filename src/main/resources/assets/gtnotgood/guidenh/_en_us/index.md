# GT-Not-Good Guide

Learn how to connect devices, operate them, and meet their requirements.

- [Wireless Packaged Pattern Provider](packaged-pattern-provider.md): target binding, pattern recording, and output collection, with a 3D Arcane Workbench example.
- [ME Hatches and Virtual Molds](me-hatches.md): pattern hatches, circuit buffers, mirrors, stocking inputs, ME outputs, data access, and virtual mold slots, with four annotated 3D scenes.
- [AE Buses, Interfaces and Terminals](ae-devices.md): stock and threshold buses, both interface variants, terminal encoding, automatic requests, and wireless tools, with five annotated 3D scenes.
- [Flux Energy and Logistics](flux-network.md): Flux Plugs, Flux Points and Flux Logistics Plugs, with three annotated 3D scenes covering wireless EU and ME restocking.

Hover over a covered device or tool in your inventory and hold GuideNH's open-guide key (default **G**) to open its tutorial.
